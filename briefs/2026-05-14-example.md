# Brief — 2026-05-14-focusblock-launch-vertical

The first worked example for this repo. Paired with the rendered output documentation in `examples.md`. Deliberately industry-neutral so the example illustrates the architecture without bleeding into any specific operator's domain. Uses the same product fiction (FocusBlock) as the worked example in `your-content-worker`, so a reader can see one product, two workers, two output formats.

---

## 1. What to produce

One 30-second vertical (9:16, 1080×1920) voice-over MP4 announcing the FocusBlock 1.0 launch. Stock-footage backing, three cuts, one title card. Burned-in captions for accessibility. No silent variant.

## 2. Audience and platform

Remote knowledge workers scrolling LinkedIn and Instagram Reels on phone. Vertical 9:16 plays full-width on phone (~390px on most devices) but is often watched muted in feed — captions are not optional. Average watch-through for this content category lands around 12-18 seconds, so the point must land before 15 seconds. The CTA is the final five seconds.

## 3. Script + script timing

70 words. Read at a natural pace, this should land between 28 and 32 seconds.

```
[00:00-00:05]  Most productivity apps optimize for willpower.

[00:05-00:11]  FocusBlock optimizes for scheduling.

[00:11-00:20]  By 2pm, the question of whether to focus
                has already been decided — by what's on
                your calendar.

[00:20-00:26]  FocusBlock puts 90-minute focus windows
                on your calendar before willpower has
                to defend anything.

[00:26-00:30]  Available today. focusblock.example/download
```

Emphasis marked by line breaks. Pauses honored.

## 4. Visual direction

```
Aspect: 9:16 (1080×1920)
Frame rate: 30 fps
Codec: h.264 high, AAC audio at 192k

Title card (0:00-0:02): Black background. "FocusBlock 1.0" in the visual-system primary type, centered, fade-in. Title card double-functions as the opening of the first script line.

Section 1 footage (0:02-0:11): Pexels search "person looking at phone morning coffee," vertical-friendly clip, 9 seconds. Slow zoom-in (1.0x to 1.05x).

Section 2 footage (0:11-0:20): Pexels search "calendar app on phone screen close up," vertical, 9 seconds. Static frame, no zoom.

Section 3 footage (0:20-0:26): Pexels search "person working at desk afternoon focused," vertical, 6 seconds. Static.

Section 4 (0:26-0:30): Black background, URL in large monospace centered, slow fade-in.

Captions: burned-in, white sans (Inter or system sans), 64px at 1080px wide, lower-third position (180px above bottom edge), 2px black shadow, max 22 chars per line, max 2 lines per cue. Held until the next caption.

Visual-system colors used: primary type from reference/visual-system.md (currently default — operator's placeholder values).
```

## 5. Voice source

Use TTS for this example. Engine: OpenAI tts-1-hd, voice: "alloy" (neutral, no specific brand voice). Reference the OPENAI_API_KEY from env, never embed.

For a real operator dispatch, swap in your TTS choice or your recorded voice file. Voice-clone is gated; don't reach for it until you've configured a consent record.

## 6. Success criteria

- Duration between 28 and 32 seconds.
- Aspect exactly 1080×1920, h.264 high, AAC at 192k.
- Captions burned in, white-with-black-shadow, readable at 320px scaled width (test by viewing at 30% zoom on desktop).
- Title card reads "FocusBlock 1.0" at the visual-system's primary font size, centered horizontally and vertically.
- All four footage cues sourced via Pexels API (configured key in env). No footage from any other source.
- CTA URL is present in the closing frame, monospace, readable.
- Audio-muxed cleanly — no clicks at cut points, no clipping on the voice-over track.
- Final MP4 file size under 15MB (Pexels footage compressed via Remotion's default h.264 settings is usually well below this).
- No invented FocusBlock capabilities. The script states only what the product brief grounds: 90-minute focus windows, calendar-based, available today.

## 7. Refusal context

N/A — no third-party voice clone (TTS only), no high-harm domain, no claims requiring external sources. All product facts in this brief are simulated for the worked example; in a real dispatch the brief would either ground the product facts itself or reference a `reference/products.md` file.

- **Product name:** FocusBlock

- **Key feature:** Blocks 90-minute focus windows on the user's calendar based on the user's actual energy patterns.
